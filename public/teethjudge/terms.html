Not medical advice

TeethJudge is an entertainment and self-improvement app. Scores are generated by an on-device image heuristic and are not a medical or dental assessment, diagnosis, or treatment recommendation. Care tips and routines are general hygiene guidance. For real dental concerns, see a dentist.

TeethJudge Pro

TeethJudge Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple and tied to your Apple ID. It unlocks unlimited scans and the Glow-Up Plan on any device signed into the same Apple ID — use Restore Purchase in Settings. It is not a subscription and never renews.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple; refund requests go through App Store support.

Your data

Everything the app stores lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes it; the in-app Erase All Data option does the same while keeping the app installed. See the Privacy Policy.

Fair use

Scores are opinions of an algorithm about pixels. Don't use TeethJudge to judge people who didn't ask. Be nice.

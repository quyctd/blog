Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-03

Summary

TeethJudge collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Photos are analyzed on your device and never leave it.

Your photos

Scans are analyzed on-device with Apple's Vision framework. The photo you scan exists in memory only for the duration of the scan — it is never uploaded, never stored on a server, and never shared. TeethJudge does not save your photos; scan history keeps only scores and dates.

What TeethJudge stores on your device

Scan scores and dates, your routine check-offs and streaks, your goal score, and your preferences (reminders on/off) are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase them anytime in Settings → Erase All Data inside the app.

Camera access

TeethJudge asks for camera access for one purpose: taking the selfie you choose to scan. You can also use a photo from your library instead — the system photo picker shares only the photo you select.

Purchases

The one-time TeethJudge Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We receive no personal information from it. The entitlement is tied to your Apple ID and restorable through the app.

Notifications

Daily routine reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server, because there is no server.

Third parties

None. No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no crash-reporting services, no data brokers.

My scan keeps failing

  • "No face detected" — get your whole face in frame, close to the camera, in good light.
  • "Show us those teeth" — smile wide; the scan needs your teeth clearly visible, not a closed-lip smile.
  • "Too dark" — face a window or turn on a light. Dim photos would score unfairly low, so the app asks for a retake instead.

I bought Pro on another device

Open Settings → Restore Purchase. Pro is tied to your Apple ID, not the device.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Erase All Data wipes scans, streaks, and routine history. Everything is stored on-device only, so deleting the app removes all data too. Your Pro purchase stays with your Apple ID either way.

Reminders aren't firing

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → TeethJudge and make sure notifications are allowed, then toggle Daily Reminders off and on inside the app.

Is my score a medical assessment?

No — it's an entertainment score from an on-device heuristic. For real dental concerns, see a dentist. Details in the Terms of Use.
